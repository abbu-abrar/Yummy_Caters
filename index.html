The Story

Catering built around the table, not the truck.

Yummy Caters plans every event the way a good host would — starting with who's coming, what they'll love, and how the evening should feel. Menus are written for the occasion, not pulled from a fixed package.

From engagement lunches to thousand-guest weddings, our kitchen and service teams work as one unit: sourcing carefully, cooking fresh on site or close to it, and serving with the kind of attention that lets the host actually enjoy their own event.

"We don't cater events. We cater memories."
